# mustelus: Chondrichthyan Fisheries Biology

Tools for analysing the life history of chondrichthyan fishes (sharks,
rays and chimaeras), covering length-weight relationships, length or age
at maturity and maternity, fecundity, clasper elongation, and von
Bertalanffy growth.
